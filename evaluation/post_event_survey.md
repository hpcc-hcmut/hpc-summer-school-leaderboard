# KHẢO SÁT TRẢI NGHIỆM HỌC VIÊN - HCMUT HPC SUMMER SCHOOL 2026

## Public instrument only

This is the public reproduction of the post-event survey instrument recovered
from the original form-response export. It contains question wording and the
response formats recoverable from that export only. It deliberately excludes
timestamps, form IDs, names, email addresses, all individual responses, and
free-text content.

The export stores numeric ratings as `1`–`5`, but does not retain the original
labels for those endpoints. Where anchors are shown below, they were
independently confirmed against the original survey; unshown endpoint labels
are not reconstructed or inferred. The recommendation question explicitly uses
the numeric `0`–`10` scale. For multi-select and categorical questions, the
response option definition was not preserved independently of participant
responses, so this document does not reconstruct or infer a full option list.

English translations below are supplied for accessibility. The Vietnamese
wording remains the primary wording recovered from the original export.
